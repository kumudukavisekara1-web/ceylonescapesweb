Island Directory

Destinations of Sri Lanka

From the ancient rock citadels of the dry northern plains to misty mountain tea gardens and palm-fringed southern shores, explore the remarkable geographic diversity of Sri Lanka.

Commercial Capital

Colombo

Sri Lanka's oceanfront metropolis blending colonial British architecture, bustling street markets in Pettah, serene Buddhist temples, and contemporary high-rise skylines along the Indian Ocean.

Main Attractions: Galle Face Green, Gangaramaya Temple, National Museum, Old Dutch Hospital, Independence Square, Pettah Floating Market.
Recommended Experiences: Sunset ocean stroll, street food tour at Galle Face, contemporary art gallery walks.
Suggested Duration: 1 – 2 Days

Sacred Hill Capital

Kandy

The last independent royal kingdom of Sri Lanka, surrounded by misty mountains and wrapped around peaceful Kandy Lake. The spiritual heart of Sinhala Buddhism and island craftsmanship.

Main Attractions: Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic (Sri Dalada Maligawa), Kandy Lake, Royal Botanical Gardens Peradeniya, Bahirawakanda Buddha statue.
Recommended Experiences: Attend the sacred Thevava evening ritual, witness traditional Kandyan drumming and dance, lakeside morning walk.
Suggested Duration: 2 – 3 Days
Enquire for Kandy Experiences →
UNESCO World Heritage

Sigiriya

The monumental 5th-century palace citadel constructed by King Kashyapa upon a sheer 200-metre granite monolith. Features ancient frescoes, a polished Mirror Wall, colossal Lion's Paws, and elaborate hydraulic water gardens.

Main Attractions: Sigiriya Rock Fortress, Lion's Paw terrace, Sigiriya Frescoes, Royal Water & Boulder Gardens, Pidurangala Rock.
Recommended Experiences: Sunrise ascent of Pidurangala for breathtaking views of Sigiriya; exploring royal pleasure gardens.
Suggested Duration: 1 – 2 Days

Sacred Cave Temple

Dambulla

The largest and best-preserved cave monastery complex in Sri Lanka, dating back to the 1st century BCE. Perched under an overhanging granite rock, five sanctuaries house 153 Buddha statues and masterwork murals.

Main Attractions: Royal Rock Temple Cave 1 to 5, Golden Temple modern monument, panoramic central plains viewpoint.
Recommended Experiences: Meditative walk through Cave 2 (Maharaja Vihara), observing rock ceiling natural drip springs.
Suggested Duration: 1 Day (conveniently paired with Sigiriya)
Enquire for Dambulla Heritage Guide →
Highland Tea Country

Nuwara Eliya

Perched at 1,868 metres above sea level amidst mist-wreathed mountain ridges, Nuwara Eliya represents the heart of Ceylon's high-elevation tea industry, characterized by cool climate, Tudor-style cottages, and tea gardens.

Main Attractions: Pedro Tea Estate & Factory, Lake Gregory, Victoria Park, Hakgala Botanical Gardens, Seetha Amman Temple.
Recommended Experiences: Pluck fresh two-leaves-and-a-bud with local tea harvesters, cupping tea tastings, boat cruise on Lake Gregory.
Suggested Duration: 2 – 3 Days
Enquire for Nuwara Eliya Tea Bungalows →
Scenic Gap & Railway

Ella

A serene mountain enclave famed for jaw-dropping valleys, cascading falls, and the iconic colonial Nine Arch Bridge. A paradise for hikers, nature photographers, and rail journey lovers.

Main Attractions: Nine Arch Bridge, Little Adam's Peak, Ella Rock, Ravana Falls & Cave, Demodara railway loop.
Recommended Experiences: Watching the blue passenger train pass across Nine Arch Bridge, hiking Little Adam's Peak at daybreak.
Suggested Duration: 2 – 3 Days
Enquire for Ella Adventure Info →
UNESCO Fortified City

Galle

A 16th-century oceanfront citadel founded by Portuguese traders and fortified by the Dutch. Its cobbled lanes preserve Dutch Reformed churches, spice merchant warehouses, and the towering white Galle Lighthouse.

Main Attractions: Galle Fort ramparts, Galle Lighthouse, Dutch Reformed Church, Maritime Museum, artisan gemstone boutiques.
Recommended Experiences: Sunset ramparts walk watching local cliff divers, artisan gelato, boutique heritage hotel stays.
Suggested Duration: 2 Days
Enquire for Galle Heritage Stays →
Watersports & Beaches

Bentota

Located along the south-western coast where the Bentota River meets the Indian Ocean, Bentota is Sri Lanka's prime beach resort hub, famed for golden sandbars, jet skiing, and Geoffrey Bawa architectural gardens.

Main Attractions: Bentota Beach sand spit, Bentota River lagoon, Lunuganga (Geoffrey Bawa country estate), Brief Garden by Bevis Bawa.
Recommended Experiences: Bentota river boat safari encountering water monitor lizards and mangroves, windsurfing, luxury Ayurveda spas.
Suggested Duration: 2 – 3 Days
Enquire for Bentota Beach Experiences →
Wild Safari Reserve

Yala

Sri Lanka's second largest and most celebrated national park, embracing thorn scrub, coastal lagoons, and monumental rock outcrops. Internationally renowned for hosting the world's dense concentration of leopards.

Main Attractions: Yala National Park (Block 1 & 5), Sithulpawwa ancient rock temple, coastal sand dunes, Patanangala rock.
Recommended Experiences: Dawn and dusk 4x4 open safari jeep game drives, bird watching at seasonal saline lagoons.
Suggested Duration: 2 Days
Enquire for Yala Wildlife Guide →
Whales & Palms

Mirissa

A laidback tropical coastal town shaped like a crescent moon, framed by the iconic Coconut Tree Hill headland. Recognized globally as one of the best locations to observe migrating Blue Whales in open ocean waters.

Main Attractions: Mirissa Beach, Coconut Tree Hill, Secret Beach, Parrot Rock, Mirissa Fisheries Harbour.
Recommended Experiences: Ethical morning whale watching cruise, sunset photography at Coconut Tree Hill, candlelit oceanfront seafood dining.
Suggested Duration: 2 – 3 Days
Enquire for Mirissa Whale Watching →
East Coast Sanctuary

Trincomalee

The jewel of Sri Lanka's eastern coast, endowed with one of the world's finest natural deep-water harbours, crystal clear waters at Nilaveli Beach, Pigeon Island National Park, and the ancient cliff-top Koneswaram Temple.

Main Attractions: Koneswaram Temple (Swami Rock), Nilaveli Beach, Pigeon Island Marine National Park, Fort Frederick.
Recommended Experiences: Snorkelling with blacktip reef sharks and sea turtles at Pigeon Island, whale watching (May to Oct).
Suggested Duration: 2 – 3 Days
Enquire for Trincomalee East Coast Tour →
Northern Cultural Hub

Jaffna

The spiritual and cultural capital of the Tamil people in Sri Lanka's northern peninsula. Enriched with golden-towered Hindu kovils, palm-fringed coastal causeways, historic Jaffna Fort, and distinct spicy culinary traditions.

Main Attractions: Nallur Kandaswamy Kovil, Jaffna Fort, Jaffna Public Library, Keerimalai Sacred Springs, Nainativu Island.
Recommended Experiences: Experience early morning puja at Nallur Kovil, sample authentic Jaffna crab curry, boat ride to remote Delft Island.
Suggested Duration: 2 – 3 Days
