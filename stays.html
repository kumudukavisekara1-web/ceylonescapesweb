Sri Lankan Hospitality & Stays

From restored 19th-century colonial tea planter residences to sustainable treehouse lodges and beach sanctuaries, discover accommodation shaped by authentic island architecture and heartfelt hospitality.

Luxury Resort
Bentota / Tangalle Coastline

Oceanfront Luxury Beach Resorts

Flagship 5-star coastal havens embracing Geoffrey Bawa's tropical modernism philosophy. Expansive oceanfront grounds with multi-tiered infinity pools, private beach access, and fine dining.

Key Facilities: Infinity ocean pools, holistic Ayurveda spas, 24-hour butler service, watersports concierge, oceanview dining pavilions.

Boutique Heritage
Nuwara Eliya / Hatton / Ella

Colonial Tea Planter Bungalows

Historic 19th-century stone residences surrounded by working tea estates. Warm fireplaces, English gardens, afternoon high tea, and dedicated private staff for an authentic heritage sojourn.

Key Facilities: Open hearth fireplaces, clawfoot bathtubs, private tea trail guides, croquet lawns, garden dining.
Enquire for Stays
Heritage Boutique
UNESCO Galle Fort

Galle Fort Heritage Mansions

Historic merchants' merchant houses restored with Dutch antiques, arched verandahs, and secluded sun-dappled interior courtyards located within walking distance of the lighthouse.

Key Facilities: Central plunge courtyards, period library, rooftop rampart viewing decks, customized walking tours.
Enquire for Stays
Eco Lodge
Sinharaja / Ella / Habarana

Sustainable Rainforest Lodges

Architecturally crafted using renewable timber, perched on stilts above rainforest floors. Zero-single-use plastic, solar-powered systems, and on-site naturalists for nocturnal forest walks.

Key Facilities: Solar hot water, certified naturalist trekking, organic farm produce, bird-watching observation hides.
Enquire for Stays
Ayurveda Sanctuary
Wadduwa / Beruwala / Kalutara

Authentic Ayurveda Wellness Retreats

Dedicated healing sanctuaries offering doctor-supervised Panchakarma and rejuvenation programmes, daily meditation sessions, and dosha-specific nutrition in quiet coastal gardens.

Key Facilities: On-site Ayurvedic clinic, herbal pharmacy, open-air yoga shala, organic medicinal herb gardens.
Enquire for Stays
Private Villa
Mirissa / Thalpe / Ahangama

Beachfront Private Villas

Exclusive-use multi-bedroom residences set in walled tropical grounds right on the sand. Fully staffed with private cooks, groundskeepers, and direct access to swimming beaches.

Key Facilities: Private plunge pools, dedicated in-villa chef, expansive lawn gardens, private beach gate.
Enquire for Stays
4-Star Comfort
Kandy / Sigiriya / Colombo

4-Star Cultural & City Hotels

Reliable, modern properties offering spacious family rooms, swimming pools, multi-cuisine restaurants, and convenient access to key archaeological and shopping sites.

Key Facilities: Swimming pools, fitness centers, buffet & à la carte dining, family connecting rooms, travel desks.

3-Star Value
Ella / Negombo / Dambulla

3-Star Leisure & Nature Guesthouses

Charming, family-operated hotels and boutique guesthouses celebrated for heartfelt local service, homecooked Sri Lankan meals, clean comfortable amenities, and authentic community connections.

Key Facilities: Air-conditioned rooms, complimentary Wi-Fi, home-cooked breakfasts, personalized local guidance.

Yala / Wilpattu National Park Borders

Wilderness Safari Tented Camps

Fully equipped canvas suites situated on the perimeter of national parks, offering night campfires under star-filled skies, gourmet bush dining, and direct access to early morning game drives.
