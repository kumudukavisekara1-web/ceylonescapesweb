About Ceylon Escapes

Discover Sri Lanka through the people, places and experiences that make this island extraordinary.

Discover · Connect · Respect
A more meaningful way to travel

One island. Many stories.

Ceylon Escapes is a digital tourism platform created to connect travellers with Sri Lanka's destinations, experiences, hospitality, culture, wildlife and cuisine.

From ancient heritage and sacred temples to tea-covered hills, wildlife-rich landscapes and warm island hospitality, we help curious travellers see more of Sri Lanka while travelling with care. Our approach brings inspiration and practical information together, making it easier to plan journeys that are memorable, respectful and rooted in place.

“To inspire meaningful travel experiences that connect visitors with Sri Lanka's natural, cultural and human heritage.”

What guides us

Our vision, mission
& values

We believe the best journeys create a positive connection between visitors, local communities and the places they explore.

01 · VISION

Meaningful travel

To inspire meaningful travel experiences that connect visitors with Sri Lanka's natural, cultural and human heritage.

02 · MISSION

Responsible discovery

To provide accessible, inspiring and responsible travel information while promoting Sri Lanka as a diverse and sustainable destination.

03 · VALUE

Authenticity

We share the character of Sri Lanka honestly, celebrating its living culture and distinctive places.

04 · VALUE

Hospitality

We welcome every traveller with warmth, clarity and a spirit of generous island hospitality.

05 · VALUE

Sustainability

We encourage choices that respect nature, support communities and protect the future of each destination.

06 · VALUE

Discovery

We make space for curiosity, from iconic landmarks to the quieter details between them.
